hapcha

Sketch haptics with your finger.

Tap a rhythm on the pad and feel it played back right away. Shape each tap and hold on a timeline. Then export it as an .ahap file for your own app.

  • Higher on the pad is stronger. Farther right is crisper.
  • Press and hold to make a long buzz. Slide while you hold to shape it.
  • Start from Apple's system haptic patterns, or from an empty pad.
  • Open .ahap files from Files, and play your sketches with Siri.

hapcha is free. It has no ads and no account.
