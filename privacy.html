Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

hapcha is made by Jeremie Lim. This policy explains what the hapcha app collects. The short version: your sketches stay on your iPhone, and the app sends only anonymous counts of what happens in it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your sketches, their names, and your settings. hapcha does not upload them.
  • Files you open in hapcha. hapcha reads the file you pick and saves its own copy on your iPhone.

Anonymous usage counts

hapcha uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to learn which parts of the app work and where people get stuck. The app sends short signals such as "the tour finished" or "a sketch was exported", with these details:

  • Simple values that the app chooses, such as the number of taps in a sketch, or the name of a built-in template.
  • The title of an error message, when one shows. Sketch names are removed first.
  • Whether your iPhone can play haptics, and whether audio hints are on.
  • Technical details: app version, iOS version, device model, and language and region settings.
  • A random ID for this install. It is hashed on your iPhone before it is sent, so it cannot be traced back to you or your Apple Account.

hapcha never sends your sketch names, sketch contents, file names, or anything you type. This data is not linked to you, not used for tracking, and not sold. We use it only to make hapcha better.

Siri and Shortcuts

hapcha tells iOS your sketch names so that Siri and the Shortcuts app can play them. Apple handles Siri requests under Apple's privacy policy. hapcha does not receive your voice.

Feedback email

Send Feedback opens a new email in your own mail app, with your app version, iPhone model, and iOS version filled in. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. I use what you send only to answer you and fix problems.

What hapcha does not have

  • No account or login.
  • No ads, and no ad tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.

Children

hapcha is safe for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
