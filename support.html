Questions

I can't feel anything.

  • Haptics need an iPhone. iPad and the Simulator cannot play them. hapcha shows a warning when this is the case.
  • Open Settings → Sounds & Haptics, and make sure System Haptics is on.
  • Low Power Mode can make haptics weaker.

How do I use my sketch in my own app?

Open the sketch, tap ⋯, then Export. You get an .ahap file. Play it in your app with Core Haptics. To see the file's text, choose AHAP JSON in the same menu.

How do I open an .ahap file in hapcha?

On Home, tap ⋯ and choose Import AHAP file. Or, in the Files app, touch and hold the file, then choose Open With → hapcha. hapcha saves a copy and does not change the original. If the file has parts that hapcha can't play, such as sounds, a banner lists them.

How do I play a sketch with Siri?

Say "Play sketch name in hapcha", for example "Play Heartbeat in hapcha". Or say "Play a sketch in hapcha" to choose from a list. The same action is in the Shortcuts app. Open hapcha once after you install it, so Siri learns your sketch names.

How do I see the tour again?

Delete hapcha and install it again. This also deletes your sketches, so export the ones you want to keep first.

Where are my sketches saved?

Only on your iPhone, inside hapcha. If you delete the app, iOS deletes them too.
